# Village Basket (working name) · One page

**The problem.** In the hill villages above Amchit (Bejjeh, Ghalboun, Aabaydat, Kfoun), and in any household without a car, getting oil, rice, detergent, milk and diapers at Jbeil supermarket prices means a 20–25 minute drive, a taxi, or a favour. A trip from Bejjeh costs about $6.90 in fuel and time (estimate). The alternative is the village mini-market's higher prices.

**The customer.** Car-less households and hill-village households buying a weekly stock-up. Simulated buyers who didn't fit that profile mostly passed: 0 of 40 bought the original pitch.

**The offer and price.** Twice a week (Tue/Fri):
- Send a list on WhatsApp by 9 p.m. the night before, or reply "same as last time".
- Staples only, bought together at Tawfeer MAX Jbeil at its quantity and carton prices. No fresh produce or meat.
- Each item costs its shelf price minus half the bulk saving, plus **$1.25 a bag**. Doorstep drop +$1.50; first bag free of fee.
- Collect 5–9 p.m. or the next morning at the village mini-market.
- A copy of the till receipt and a line-by-line slip in every bag. Hand back anything wrong or damaged and you don't pay for it.

**The numbers** (from `business-plan.md`, computed by the plan tool):

"A day" is a run day: 9 runs a month. The $2,975 includes a **$2,200 float that comes back**; sunk spend is $775. The founder drives; a paid driver turns year 1 negative.

**The evidence.**
- **Panel:** the v2 offer took simulated buyers from 6% (3 of 50) to 25% (5 of 20). Trust and price objections nearly disappeared.
- **Competitor gap:** nobody pools village orders in the Jbeil district. Delivery apps either don't publish fees for Amchit or don't cover it (`competitors.md`).
- **Real test:** not yet run. Three tests before launch (`launch.md`): the tier gap measured on the shelf, 25 founding households signed at the real price, and two hand-run friends-and-family runs.

**What the money is for.** $300 registration and accountant. $250 crates, cool boxes and a label printer. $175 launch flyers and first-bag fee waivers. $50 business SIM. $2,200 float to pay the till before households settle.

**The ask.** From Tawfeer MAX Jbeil: written confirmation of the quantity/carton thresholds for ~40 staples, a business invoice account, pre-picking of an order sent by 08:00, and weekly settlement. In return: an 8-week, 4-village pilot, with numbers shared every two weeks (`tawfeer-pitch.md`).
