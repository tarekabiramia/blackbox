Village Basket: a new daily grocery service for Amchit and the villages above it (Bejjeh, Ghalboun, Aabaydat, Kfoun).
Send your shopping list on WhatsApp, as text or a voice note, before 2 p.m.
We pool everyone's orders and buy them together at Tawfeer MAX Jbeil and the other Jbeil supermarkets, comparing prices and using bulk and carton prices that one household usually can't get.
Collect your bag the same evening between 6 and 8 p.m. at a pickup point in your village. There is no home delivery.
You pay what we paid at the till for your items, plus a $2 service fee per bag, in cash or by Whish at pickup.
