Village Basket: a twice-weekly grocery run for Amchit and the villages above it (Bejjeh, Ghalboun, Aabaydat, Kfoun), run by a neighbour from Amchit.
Every Tuesday and Friday. Send your list on WhatsApp, as text or a voice note, by 9 p.m. the night before, or just reply "same as last time". We send back each item and an estimated total before we shop.
Staples only: packaged food, dairy, cleaning and baby items, bought together at Tawfeer MAX Jbeil at its quantity and carton prices. We don't pick fresh vegetables or meat; you keep choosing those yourself.
You pay the normal single-item shelf price minus half of the bulk saving, plus $1.25 a bag, and no fee on your first bag. Every bag comes with the till receipt and a slip showing the shelf price, what we paid and what you saved.
Collect it from 5 to 9 p.m. or the next morning at the partner mini-market in your village, or have it left at your door for $1.50 more.
Check your bag when you collect it: anything wrong or damaged, hand it back and you don't pay for it. Pay cash or Whish; after your third bag you can settle once a week.
