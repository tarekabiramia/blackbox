# Buyer panel: Village Basket (working name): daily pooled grocery run with a village pickup point, Amchit and the Jbeil hill villages

**3 buy · 47 pass** (6% buy) out of 50 simulated buyers. Seed 2026, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- A neighbour I know and trust who has used it for a month and shows me the receipt next to the bag, and the fee dropped to $1 or free on a big weekly bag.
- If they let me pay at the end of the month, showed me the receipt, and I saw a few neighbours using it and saving real money on cartons.
- If I could send a photo of what I want, or they sent me a picture before buying, and I could refuse what I don't like at pickup.
- Delivery to my door with no fee, or proof that the carton prices come out clearly cheaper than what I pay at Tawfeer, enough to cover the fee.
- If I could see the actual till receipts and it saved me clearly more than the fee on my weekly list, and I could send one list for the week instead of every day, I might try it once when the car is off the road.
- Clear proof, with a real receipt, that my usual basket costs more than $2 less than when I buy it myself, or no fee at all on the first few orders.
- Delivery to my door and a monthly tab like my neighbour gives me, with no fee on small bags.
- Home delivery to my door, or a pickup point on my own street with a wider window, plus receipts shown for every bag.
- If they accepted payment at the end of the month like the dekkene, the fee was well under a dollar, and the pickup point was on my street.
- If it were credit like the dekkene (pay at month end) or the fee were waived, and I could see the savings on my usual items first.
- Seeing the till receipt photo for every order, plus a neighbour I know vouching for them and a first order with no fee.
- If I could see the till receipt and send back anything wrong or bad for free, and the carton prices really came out clearly under what I pay myself, I might try one bag.

Buyers say they would buy **3.0 times** in the first month on average.
