# Market research: Amchit and the Jbeil hills (Lebanon), November 2026

Research for "Village Basket" (daily pooled grocery run, WhatsApp orders until about 2 p.m., one driver run a day, collection at a pickup point in each village). Compiled 17 November 2026 from public sources only.

**How to read this file**
- Every figure links to its source, with the source's date. "Accessed 17 Nov 2026" means I opened the page that day.
- **[ESTIMATE]** marks my own calculation. The reasoning is next to it. Treat these as starting points to check on the ground, not as facts.
- **[NOT VERIFIED]** marks a figure I saw only in a search summary and could not open at the source.
- Drive times come from the free OpenStreetMap router (OSRM), which assumes empty roads. They are **estimates**. Real door-to-door times on Jbeil roads are longer (traffic on the coastal highway, slow mountain roads, parking). Where I give an "adjusted" time I added 30%, which is my assumption.
- Limits of this research: Localiban (localiban.org) and several job and rental sites could not be reached from this session, and the shared web-search budget ran out before I finished the driver-pay, van-rental, shop-rent and WhatsApp-statistics questions. Those gaps are flagged where they occur.

## 0. The five findings that matter most

1. **Amchit itself is not far from big supermarkets.** Amchit's centre is about 4.6 km and about 5–6 minutes (OSRM, empty roads) from **Spinneys Jbeil** (Mastita road) and from **Le Charcutier Aoun** (Qartaboun, coastal highway). Since May 2026 **Tawfeer Max Jbeil** has also been open in Qartaboun. For Amchit households, "going down to the city" is a 10–15 minute errand, not a trip. The selling point inside Amchit has to be **price (pooled multi-buy tiers) and time saved**, not distance.
2. **The strongest market is the hill villages 15–28 minutes away**: the Bejjeh / Ghalboun / Aabaydat / Kfoun cluster to the north-east, and the larger high-mountain villages of Ehmej, Jaj, Mechmech, Lehfed and Mayfouq. The villages that border Amchit (Gharfine, Edde, Hosrayel, Jeddayel) are only 6–9 minutes from Spinneys and are weak on the distance argument.
3. **Tawfeer Max Jbeil already sells on volume tiers.** It has a three-tier per-unit price ladder (PV1/PV2/PV3), where the top tier is aimed at small businesses ([Beirut.com, 22 May 2026](https://www.beirut.com/en/?p=775677); [Beiruting, 15 May 2026](https://www.beiruting.com/news/141422/tawfeer-max-a-new-era-of-value-retail-in-lebanon)). This is the "buy 3, get a discount" mechanism the founder wants to pool orders into, and the branch is about 5 minutes from Amchit.
4. **Money in a village household is tight and mostly cash.** Median household income in 2025 was about **USD 550 a month** ([Mercy Corps/LCAT, May 2026](https://www.prosperglobal.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/from-hormuz-to-the-frontlines-of-hunger-lebanon-country-brief.pdf), citing MSNA 2025). The 2023 average food spend was **USD 155 a month**, 34% of all spending ([REACH MSNA 2023](https://repository.impact-initiatives.org/document/reach/9e4ae296/REACH_Factsheet-MSNA-2023_Lebanese_HHs.pdf)). Whish-to-Whish and OMT Pay wallet-to-wallet transfers are free, but cash remains the default.
5. **The legal model decides the VAT exposure.** VAT registration is now mandatory from **LBP 5 billion of turnover (about USD 55,900)** ([PwC tax summaries, reviewed 1 Jul 2026](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/corporate/other-taxes)). If the business resells groceries, the whole basket value counts towards that threshold. If it buys as a fee-paid agent, only the fee may count. This needs confirming with a Lebanese accountant before launch.

**Layout.** Amchit runs from the coast (the highway and seafront) up the first slopes. Per OpenStreetMap municipal boundaries (my calculation: shared boundary vertices), Amchit **borders Jbeil to the south, Edde to the south-east, Gharfine to the east, Hosrayel to the north-east and Jeddayel to the north**. Mounsef (about 0.9 km away) and Kfar Mashoun (about 0.8 km) are close but do not share a border. I found no public source describing Amchit's internal hamlets beyond Kfar Saleh and "Hay El Dahr", the address of one supermarket ([Kompass listing](https://lb.kompass.com/c/supermarket-hajj-sarl/lb026712/), seen via search summary).

**Context for the wider area.** Jbeil caza had about **129,500 residents** in mid-2018 and an average household size of **3.7**. 13% of residents were aged 65+, against a national 11% ([CAS/ILO LFHLCS 2018-19 summary](https://lebanon.un.org/ar/download/59460/107785)). That gives **[ESTIMATE] about 35,000 households in the caza**. The older population is relevant: elderly residents of hill villages are the people for whom the drive down is hardest.

**Recommendation for the first test [ESTIMATE, judgement]:** **Bejjeh, Ghalboun, Aabaydat and Kfoun (cluster A)**, optionally with Hsarat. They are far enough from the big stores (15–19 min, about 20–25 min in real traffic), compact enough for one run, and 13–16 minutes from Amchit. As a second step, test **Ehmej / Mechmech / Jaj / Lehfed** in a summer month, when their population is largest. Before committing, count actual winter households with each village's mukhtar or municipality, since the voter roll is the only number available.

### 3.2 Car ownership
- Lebanon had **295 road motor vehicles per 1,000 people** (1,866,407 vehicles) in 2016 (WHO data via [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_territories_by_motor_vehicles_per_capita)). **[ESTIMATE]** That is roughly one vehicle per household (295 × 3.8 persons ÷ 1,000 ≈ 1.1), including commercial vehicles.
- I found no current household-level car-ownership figure for Jbeil. Ask about car access in the customer survey: households with no car, or with one car that the working member takes to the job, are the natural first customers.

### 3.3 Worked example: one supermarket trip **[ESTIMATE]**

Assumptions (mine): a typical car uses 9 L/100 km on hill roads (range 7–11); 95 octane at USD 1.31/L; 45 minutes of parking, shopping and queueing; driving time = OSRM × 1.3; time valued at the minimum wage (LBP 28M ÷ 208 h ≈ **USD 1.50/h**). Wear, tyres and depreciation are not included.

| Household in… | Round trip | Fuel | Time | Fuel + time per trip | Per month at 1 trip/week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bejjeh** (far hill village) | 37.8 km, about 50 min driving | **USD 4.45** (3.46–5.44) | about 1.6 h ≈ USD 2.40 | **≈ USD 6.9** (5.9–7.8) | ≈ USD 30 |
| Ehmej / Jaj (high mountain) | about 45–49 km, about 60–70 min driving | ≈ USD 5.3–5.8 | about 1.8–2 h ≈ USD 2.9 | ≈ USD 8–9 | ≈ USD 35–38 |
| **Amchit centre** | 9.2 km, about 14 min driving | **USD 1.08** (0.84–1.32) | about 1 h ≈ USD 1.50 | **≈ USD 2.6** | ≈ USD 11 |

**What this means for pricing:** a per-order fee of USD 3–5 is below what a far-village household already spends per trip, but above what an Amchit-centre household spends. In Amchit, the case has to be made on the multi-buy savings captured and on the hour saved.

## 5. Payments in 2026

- **Cash still dominates.** The World Bank estimated Lebanon's dollarised cash economy at **45.7% of GDP (USD 9.9 bn) in 2022** ([World Bank press release, 16 May 2023](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2023/05/16/lebanon-normalization-of-crisis-is-no-road-to-stabilization)). No newer official estimate was found. Cash on delivery is "still core" in Lebanese e-commerce, and Spinneys and Carrefour offer cash or card on delivery ([LeBusiness, 29 Mar 2026](https://leb.business/en/article/lebanon-s-e-commerce-landscape-in-2026-the-players-logistics-rails-and-payment-methods), editorial). Households hold both USD and LBP. Prices are usually quoted in USD and change is often given in LBP.
- **Whish Money** (licensed by BDL, Resolution 19/21/22 of 10 Aug 2022). Fee schedule accessed 17 Nov 2026 ([whish.money/fees-commissions](https://whish.money/fees-commissions)):
  - Whish-to-Whish transfers: **free**. Cash-in: free.
  - Cash-out over the counter: free up to LBP 100M, 1% above that. USD cash-out: 1%.
  - Local USD transfer to a cash pick-up: USD 1 for 1–100, USD 2 for 101–200, and so on. Payment link: 3%.
  - Balance limits: **individual USD 3,000**, corporate USD 30,000.
  - Whish Pay merchant fee: **1%** (2% on Shopify), with no customer fee ([LeBusiness, 5 Jun 2026](https://leb.business/en/article/whish-money-vs-omt-which-pays-better-for-lebanese-merchants)). Over 2 million users (same article). QR "Scan to Pay" works in LBP and USD.
- **OMT Pay:** dual-currency wallet; QR payments in shops; **wallet-to-wallet and cash top-up at OMT locations are free** for customers; 1,400+ locations. OMT "Intra" transfer: USD 1 for USD 1–100 (from 1 Nov 2025). OMT does not publish its merchant rates ([LeBusiness, 5 Jun 2026](https://leb.business/en/article/whish-money-vs-omt-which-pays-better-for-lebanese-merchants)).
- **BOB Finance, MyMonty, cards:** MyMonty is a BDL-authorised multi-currency wallet ([LeBusiness, 29 Mar 2026](https://leb.business/en/article/lebanon-s-e-commerce-landscape-in-2026-the-players-logistics-rails-and-payment-methods)). No 2026 fee data was found for BOB Finance. Stripe needs a foreign entity, so local merchants use wallets, cash and local gateways (Areeba, Tap).
- **Wallet regulation:** BDL basic decision 13790 on electronic payment services ([BDL PDF](https://www.bdl.gov.lb/CB%20Com/Laws%20And%20Regulations/Basic%20Circulars/Decision_13790_EN%C2%A711334_3.pdf), not opened) requires providers to keep 90% of wallet funds at banks in Lebanon (per a secondary summary, **[NOT VERIFIED]**).
- **For Village Basket:**
  - Customers can pay by **Whish or OMT wallet-to-wallet at zero fee**, or in cash at the pickup point.
  - A sole trader on an **individual Whish account hits the USD 3,000 balance cap quickly** (one week of baskets). A corporate account (cap USD 30,000) probably requires a registered business; onboarding rules were not found, so ask Whish and OMT.
  - Village shops extend credit: 20% of indebted households cite a supermarket owner as a creditor ([MSNA 2023](https://repository.impact-initiatives.org/document/reach/9e4ae296/REACH_Factsheet-MSNA-2023_Lebanese_HHs.pdf)). Decide early whether to allow "pay on pickup" and how to stop bad debt.

## 7. Context risks in 2026 (brief)

- **War since 2 March 2026.** Hezbollah fired on Israel after US–Israeli strikes on Iran, and Israel launched large-scale operations across Lebanon ([Wikipedia, Timeline of the 2026 Lebanon war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_the_2026_Lebanon_war)).
  - A US-brokered Israel–Lebanon cessation of hostilities took effect on 16 April 2026 and was extended several times. A framework deal was announced on 26 June, which Hezbollah rejected ([Wikipedia, 2026 Israel–Lebanon ceasefire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israel%E2%80%93Lebanon_ceasefire)).
  - By 31 August 2026, OCHA counted about 334,000 people still displaced and 823,000 returned. Airstrikes and demolitions continued in the South and Nabatieh ([OCHA Flash Update #52](https://www.unocha.org/publications/report/lebanon/lebanon-flash-update-52-escalation-hostilities-lebanon-31-august-2026), seen via search summary **[NOT VERIFIED]**).
  - As of 17 Nov 2026, ceasefire violations in the south and pressure on Hezbollah to disarm continue; the UNIFIL mandate expires on 31 December ([Drop Site News, 17 Nov 2026](https://dropsitenews.com/p/forensic-scientist-epstein-jeffries-house-leadership-eu-rwanda-migrants), via search summary).
- **Jbeil district specifically.** The sources reviewed record strikes on "Mount Lebanon" on 4 March 2026 without naming places, and **no incident in Jbeil district or Amchit** in the parts I could read. I could not confirm that none happened. Amchit has a Shia minority. An Israeli advocacy centre has alleged a Hezbollah-linked presence in the town ([Alma, Jun 2023](https://israel-alma.org/aamchit-hezbollahs-civilian-entrenchment-in-a-christian-area-on-its-way-to-establishing-a-military-entrenched-stronghold/)); the claim is contested and is noted only as a security-risk factor. Displaced families sheltered in Mount Lebanon during the war, which raises local demand but strains services ([ACAPS](https://www.acaps.org/en/countries/archives/detail/lebanon-escalation-of-conflict-and-forced-displacement-in-southern-areas)).
- **Economy.** The World Bank projects **real GDP −6.4% in 2026** after +4.2% in 2025, driven by a tourism collapse and weaker consumption ([Al Jazeera, 22 Aug 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/22/world-bank-projects-war-hit-lebanons-economy-to-contract-by-6-4-percent)). Inflation was 17.5% in September 2026 and food inflation 10.0% ([Trading Economics/CAS](https://tradingeconomics.com/lebanon/food-inflation)). The LBP peg is holding at 89,500 ([Mercy Corps, May 2026](https://www.prosperglobal.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/from-hormuz-to-the-frontlines-of-hunger-lebanon-country-brief.pdf)).
- **Supply.** Cargo calls at Lebanese ports fell 25% between February and March 2026, and imported meat prices rose 50–80% ([Mercy Corps, May 2026](https://www.prosperglobal.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/from-hormuz-to-the-frontlines-of-hunger-lebanon-country-brief.pdf)). Expect supermarket offers to change often, which helps a price-comparison service but makes quoted prices go stale quickly.
- **For a small business in Jbeil:** fuel-price swings, the risk of a renewed escalation closing roads or cutting footfall, and seasonal population swings in the mountain villages (summer peak). Keep fixed costs near zero (no lease, no van purchase) until demand is proven.

## 8. WhatsApp usage and ordering by WhatsApp

- **Connectivity:** 5.38 million internet users (**91.8%** of the population), 4.76 million mobile connections (81.3%), 4.58 million social-media user identities (78.1%). Facebook ad reach is 3.45 million and Instagram 2.95 million ([DataReportal, Digital 2026: Lebanon, 8 Nov 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-lebanon)).
- **A WhatsApp-specific penetration figure for Lebanon (2025–2026) was not found.** DataReportal publishes none for Lebanon, and the search budget ran out before other sources (Arab Barometer, Northwestern Qatar media-use surveys) could be checked. Indirect evidence of how central it is: in October 2019 a proposed **USD 0.20 a day charge on WhatsApp/VoIP calls** helped trigger nationwide protests and was withdrawn within hours ([Wikipedia, 2019–2021 Lebanese protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%932021_Lebanese_protests)).
- **Ordering groceries by WhatsApp is already normal:**
  - Yala Stop (35 stores) ran a call centre reachable "via the phone or WhatsApp" ([Berytech, 29 Nov 2021](https://berytech.org/yala-stop-convenience-in-store-online/)).
  - "Many merchants" still take orders by DM or WhatsApp and confirm payment by screenshot or cash ([LeBusiness, 29 Mar 2026](https://leb.business/en/article/lebanon-s-e-commerce-landscape-in-2026-the-players-logistics-rails-and-payment-methods); editorial observation, unsourced).

Whether either delivers to the hill villages was not verified; see competitors.md.
- **Implication:** WhatsApp ordering adds no new habit for customers; it is already how they talk to shops. The differentiation has to be the pooled price and the reliable daily run, not the channel.
