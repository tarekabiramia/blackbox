# Meeting Tawfeer: the partnership one-pager

For the founder's first conversation with Tawfeer (Tawfeer International SAL; the branch is Tawfeer MAX Jbeil, Qartaboun). Facts are from `research/suppliers.md`: 41 branches as of April 2026, a stated target of 500 stores by 2030, "Franchise" and "Manchise" investment models, the three-tier PV1/PV2/PV3 pricing at MAX, and customer service on 1626 / customerservice@tawfeer.market.

## The frame: you are a channel, not a discount hunter

Don't ask "give me cheaper prices". MAX already sells carton prices to small businesses with no membership, so the price is public. Ask for the **service around the price**, and offer something Tawfeer can't easily get alone: **pooled, pre-ordered demand from hill villages that don't shop at MAX Jbeil today, delivered through pickup points in existing village shops.** That turns the board's biggest threat ("Tawfeer could do this itself") into the reason it should work with you. Tawfeer wants reach and has a 500-store target; you offer reach without a store.

## What you bring (say it in this order)

1. **Predictable volume.** One consolidated order twice a week (Tue/Fri), sent the night before, all paid at the till. You give a number after the pilot, not before.
2. **New customers, not cannibalised ones.** The target is car-less households and the villages 20–25 minutes up the hill (Bejjeh, Ghalboun, Aabaydat, Kfoun), people who mostly don't make the trip to Qartaboun.
3. **The Tawfeer name on every bag.** Every customer gets the Tawfeer MAX receipt and a slip showing Tawfeer's shelf price. It is free marketing into villages.
4. **Data Tawfeer doesn't have:** what a village buys, item by item, twice a week. Share an anonymised monthly summary.

## What you ask for (most important first)

| # | the ask | why it matters to you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Confirm the PV2/PV3 thresholds** for your top ~40 items, and whether a **mixed carton** counts toward PV3 | Half your revenue per bag is your share of this gap (`cfo.md`) |
| 2 | **A business account with an invoice** (VAT number on the invoice) | Clean books; the agent-vs-reseller question (`research/market-lebanon.md` §6) |
| 3 | **Pre-picking:** you send the order by 08:00 and collect at ~10:00 | Saves an hour or more of in-store picking per run |
| 4 | **Weekly settlement** or a small credit line after a trial period | Shrinks the $2,200 cash float (`cfo.md`) |
| 5 | **Permission** to print "bought at Tawfeer MAX Jbeil" and its shelf prices on your slips | Your trust promise depends on it |
| 6 | A **weekly price list** for those 40 items (a PDF or WhatsApp is fine) | Replaces any need to scrape |

**Don't ask for:** exclusivity, prices below PV3, or a rebate in the first meeting. Raise the rebate after the pilot, with the numbers.

## The pilot to propose

- **8 weeks, 4 villages, twice-weekly runs, Tawfeer MAX Jbeil as the only supplier.**
- **Every 2 weeks you share:** bags, total value, top SKUs, and the share of customers who say they don't usually shop at MAX.
- **Success line (agree it up front):** 20 bags a run by week 8 (`cfo.md` plan).
- **After 8 weeks, two possible next conversations:** (a) a volume rebate, or (b) "Tawfeer Village Pickup" as a formal channel, with you operating it and your software running the orders. That is where licensing to Tawfeer, not to individual villagers, becomes the bigger prize (`board.md`, Monopoly lens: "a buying network, not a licence").

## Protect yourself

- Don't hand over the software, the host-shop network or customer phone numbers. Share aggregate data only.
- Get anything about tiers, pre-picking or settlement in writing: email or a WhatsApp from a named manager.
- Have a backup supplier ready (Spinneys Jbeil or Le Charcutier for out-of-stocks; `research/suppliers.md`).

## Opening lines (Lebanese Arabic)

> مرحبا، أنا [الاسم] من عمشيت. عم بشتغل على خدمة بتجمع طلبيات البيوت بالضيع اللي فوق جبيل، بشري، غلبون، العبيدات وكفون، وبجيبها مرتين بالجمعة من توفير ماكس جبيل، وبيستلموها من دكانة الضيعة. ما عم أطلب حسومات خاصة. بدي أفهم كيف بتشتغل أسعار الكمية والكرتونة على طلبية مجمّعة، وإذا فيكن تحضّرولي الطلبية مسبقاً بفاتورة باسم الشركة. بالمقابل، كل زبون عندي بياخد فاتورة توفير مع كيسه، وبتوصلوا لزبائن ما عم بينزلوا عليكن هلق. بقترح تجربة ٨ أسابيع وبشارككن الأرقام كل أسبوعين.

(In English: "I'm from Amchit. I'm building a service that pools household orders from the villages above Jbeil and buys them twice a week at Tawfeer MAX Jbeil, with pickup at the village shop. I'm not asking for special discounts. I want to understand how quantity and carton prices apply to a pooled order, and whether you can pre-pick it on a company invoice. In return, every customer gets a Tawfeer receipt in their bag, and you reach customers who don't come down to you today. I'm proposing an 8-week pilot, and I'll share the numbers every two weeks.")
