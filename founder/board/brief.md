# Board brief: Village Basket (working name)

You are reviewing a business idea before any money is spent. This brief is everything the founder said, organised. It is the founder's pitch, not an endorsement.

## The founder
An R&D full-stack developer in Lebanon who recently builds 3D websites and is an AI enthusiast. He will build all the software himself (no dev cost). He lives in Amchit (عمشيت), a town in the Jbeil (Byblos) district of Mount Lebanon, which has several neighbouring villages. He says he has limited liquidity: he cannot cover many villages at once.

## The idea, in his words, organised
1. **Daily order window.** Residents of the village send what they need, mostly by WhatsApp ("he just sends us what he needs"), up to a daily cut-off, for example 2 p.m.
2. **Consolidation.** After the cut-off the software combines all orders into one batch.
3. **Supplier #1: Tawfir Shop.** A Lebanese store with many offers of the type "buy 3 of an item and get a big discount". Pooling many households' orders lets the business hit those multi-buy thresholds that a single household would not. The founder plans to approach Tawfir's owner, not to pitch the idea but to ask for a direct supplier ("peer-to-peer") relationship so he can source his products from Tawfir.
4. **Price comparison agent.** An AI agent compares prices across 5 or 6 good candidate supermarkets (it may scrape other supermarkets' websites for the needed products). If an item is cheaper elsewhere, buy it there. Batches are split per store.
5. **Owner dashboard.** Tracks everything for the owners (orders, batches, suppliers, money).
6. **Driver view.** The driver going to the market has his own view listing everything to pick at each store, to shop fast.
7. **Pickup point.** A central place in each village where people collect their bags, instead of everyone driving "down to the city" for good prices.
8. **How it makes money: undecided.** Options he named: a subscription, a percentage on each invoice, "or whatever". He wants it researched.
9. **Long term.** Sell, license or rent the software to other villages or operators outside Amchit; market it as "I run this in my own village, here is the whole system". Possibly scale to more villages himself later, but not now.

## What is NOT known yet (other skills will answer)
- Real prices and offers at Tawfir and other stores; whether Tawfir will deal with him.
- How many households would order, how often, and basket size.
- Costs: driver, fuel, pickup point, packaging, payment handling.
- Who already does something like this nearby (delivery apps, the village mini-market, informal WhatsApp groups).
- The fee buyers would accept.

## Context a stranger needs
- Lebanon's economy is largely dollarised since the 2019 crisis; cash (USD) and local money-transfer wallets are common, card use is lower than in Europe/US.
- WhatsApp is the default way people message businesses in Lebanon.
- "Going down to the city" means driving to the coastal town (Jbeil/Byblos or further) where the big supermarkets are.
